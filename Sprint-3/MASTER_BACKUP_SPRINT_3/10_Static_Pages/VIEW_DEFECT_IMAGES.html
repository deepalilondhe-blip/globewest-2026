DEFECT 1: Help Centre Page Missing <title> Tag (WCAG Violation) & 404 Hero Banner
P1 - HIGH
Affected Page Help Centre / FAQ (https://mcstaging2.globewest.com/help-centre)
Expected (AU Baseline) Browser title is '<title>Help centre</title>' (conforms to WCAG 2.2 AA Criterion 2.4.2 Page Titled). Hero banner image loads crisp photography.
Actual (US Staging Live) Browser title is completely EMPTY (<title></title> / document.title === ""). Hero banner image help-center-banner.jpg returns HTTP 404.
Root Cause & Impact SEO & Accessibility defect. Screen readers and search crawlers cannot identify page purpose without a title tag. Broken banner causes visible image pop-in/missing graphic.
DEFECT 2: US Contact Us Page Leaking Australian Showrooms & .com.au Emails
P1 - HIGH
Affected Page Contact Us (https://mcstaging2.globewest.com/contact-us)
Expected US Showroom locations and US trade customer service contacts (or generic inquiry form).
Actual (US Staging Live) Displays Australian Showroom addresses (Melbourne Head Office, Sydney, Brisbane, Perth) and Australian contact emails (melbourne@globewest.com.au, etc.). Broken image: GW-090724-005-3_375x420.jpg.
Root Cause & Impact CMS catalog copy was cloned from AU production without US showroom localization. US customers will see Australian physical addresses and email domains.
DEFECT 3: Australian Corporate Entity & Statutory Jurisdiction Leaked in US Terms
P2 - MEDIUM
Affected Page Terms & Conditions (https://mcstaging2.globewest.com/terms-and-conditions-page) & Privacy Policy
Expected Terms governed by appropriate US jurisdiction, US entity information, and US claims/support channels.
Actual (US Staging Live) Clause 1.1 specifies: "GlobeWest Pty Ltd, Victoria, Australia with company number ACN 674 872 621", lists claims@globewest.com.au, and references PPSA (Australian Personal Property Securities Act).
Root Cause & Impact Legal copy needs legal team review to ensure US entity registration and governing state law replace Australian corporate references.
DEFECT 4: 404 Error Page Document Title Leaking Staging Prefix '[US]'
P3 - LOW
Affected Page 404 Error Page (https://mcstaging2.globewest.com/non-existent-page-404-test)
Expected (AU Baseline) Clean page title: <title>404 Not Found</title>.
Actual (US Staging Live) Document title renders as <title>[US] 404 Not Found</title> leaking internal staging store view name.
Root Cause & Impact Magento CMS page configuration for 404 in US store view retains the staging store code prefix [US] in Page Title field.
