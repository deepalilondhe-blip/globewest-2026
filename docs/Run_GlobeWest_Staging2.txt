====================================================
 ⚑ MANUAL CHECK PAGES — Need Human Tester
====================================================

  STEP 10 — PAGE: Shopping Cart
  ─────────────────────────────
  • Fields: "Name Your Order" + "Client Name"
  • Why manual: These B2B fields only appear when
    logged in as a B2B customer account.
    A guest session does NOT show these fields.
  • What to check with NVDA:
    - Tab to "Name Your Order" field
    - NVDA should say: "Name Your Order, edit"
    - Type text — NVDA should echo each character
    - Tab to "Client Name" field
    - NVDA should say: "Client Name, edit"

  STEP 17 — PAGE: Checkout — Review & Payment
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────
  • Button: "Place Order"
  • Why manual: The Place Order button is DISABLED
    until a Reseller is pre-assigned in Staging 2
    database for postcode 3000.
    Ask developer to configure this first.
  • What to check with NVDA:
    - Tab to Terms & Conditions checkbox
    - NVDA should say: "Terms and conditions, checkbox, not checked"
    - Press Space to check it
    - NVDA should say: "checked"
    - Tab to Place Order button
    - NVDA should say: "Place Order, button"
    - Press Space or Enter to activate

  STEP 18 — PAGE: Order Confirmation (Success Page)
  ──────────────────────────────────────────────────
  • URL: /checkout/onepage/success/
  • Why manual: Depends on Step 17 completion.
  • What to check with NVDA:
    - NVDA should announce page heading:
      "Thank you for your purchase"
    - Order number should be read aloud
    - "Continue Shopping" link should be reachable
