Thank you so much for the detailed feedback and for pointing out these design aspects. Noted on all points—we will ensure we are extra conscious and thorough in inspecting visual details, spacing, and element placement against Figma across all storefront tickets going forward.

We have reviewed each of your points in detail alongside the approved Figma designs (`desktop/Category/Trade Pricing`) and the live US Staging site. Please find our updates below:

1. Compare Checkbox:
   • Spacing & Background Context: In the approved Figma designs, both the product card and the product photography share a seamless pure white background (#FFFFFF). Because there is no visible line or container boundary in Figma, there is no separate inset gap between the checkbox and the image in the design (as shown in attached Figma Screenshots 1 & 2). On Staging, the grey background of the temporary NetSuite placeholder cards ("GW Coming Soon") created an artificial edge contrast, making the checkbox appear flush against a border. On actual products with white photography, the alignment naturally matches Figma.
   • Typography & Font Weight: We verified the font styling on US Staging, and the Compare text is set to regular weight (400), which directly corresponds to the regular font used in Figma (see attached Verified Screenshot 3). It is not bolded.

2. New Tag & Badges:
   • Placement Below Image: We completely accept this point—we missed this detail during our earlier retesting, and we really appreciate you catching it! Developer @VinodV has updated this on US Staging: the badges ([New], [Customisable]) have been moved out of the image container and now sit comfortably below the product image in the details section, directly above the product title.
   • Pill Capsule Styling: The badges are now displayed in proper rounded pill capsule shapes with comfortable internal spacing, matching the Figma design specifications (see attached Verified Screenshot 3).

3. Pricing & Stock Messaging Across Products:
   • For the US B2B storefront, wholesale pricing and live stock indicators are architected around the customer login state to protect trade margins.
   • In Public / Guest browsing mode (logged out), pricing and stock are intentionally masked and unpriced across the catalog.
   • Once logged in with an authorized Trade Customer account (e.g., deepali.londhe@overdose.digital), all product cards dynamically display the dual pricing line (Wholesale Trade Price + MSRP, e.g., $1,094.50 • MSRP: $2,271.50) as well as the inventory status (• In Stock / ETA dates), exactly as designed.
